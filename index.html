📋 Operational Playbook: Grant Acquisition Roadmap

Step-by-step master checklist for acquiring federal, corporate, and state grants for Native American campus AI infrastructure.

Phase 1: Institutional & Tribal Readiness (Weeks 1 - 3)

  • SAM.gov Registration: Register institution or Tribe in SAM.gov to secure active Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) and CAGE code.
  • Tribal Resolution / Leadership Approval: Obtain formal Tribal Council Resolution or Board Authorization approving grant submission.
  • Data Sovereignty Agreement: Draft institutional governance policy ensuring sovereign ownership of locally hosted AI models and datasets.

Phase 2: Technical Design & Vendor Quotes (Weeks 4 - 6)

  • Hardware Specs & Quotes: Solicit itemized vendor quotes for GPU servers, NVMe storage arrays, high-speed switches, and UPS backup.
  • Facility Site Audit: Verify campus electrical capacity (208V/240V Dedicated Circuit), HVAC cooling capacity, and physical security.
  • Project Narrative: Write proposal connecting AI server infrastructure directly to Native student retention, STEM research, and workforce development.

Phase 3: Formal Application Submission (Weeks 7 - 8)

  • Federal Portal Submission: Submit proposal via Grants.gov or Research.gov (for NSF TCUP) before 5:00 PM EST deadline.
  • Corporate & Private Portals: Apply to NVIDIA Hardware Grant Portal, Microsoft TSI, or Sloan Foundation LOI portal.
  • Post-Submission Follow-Up: Contact assigned Federal Program Officer or Corporate Liaison for confirmation of receipt.
