# Sample Tribal Council Resolution Template

**RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE [NAME OF TRIBAL NATION / TRIBE]**

**RESOLUTION NO.: [INSERT RESOLUTION NUMBER]**

**SUBJECT: Authorization to Apply for Federal, State, and Corporate Grant Funding for On-Campus Artificial Intelligence (AI) Server Infrastructure and STEM Educational Expansion**

---

### **WHEREIN:**

**WHEREAS**, the [Name of Tribal Nation] is a federally recognized Indian Tribe organized under [applicable Constitution / Tribal Code / Treaty], possessing inherent sovereignty and authority over its tribal lands, educational institutions, and tribal members; and

**WHEREAS**, the Tribal Council is the duly elected governing body of the [Name of Tribal Nation], authorized to enact laws, approve grant submissions, and oversee economic, technological, and educational development for the welfare of the Tribe; and

**WHEREAS**, access to advanced Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) education, High-Performance Computing (HPC), and Artificial Intelligence (AI) infrastructure is vital for the academic success, career readiness, and technological self-determination of Native American youth and college students; and

**WHEREAS**, the [Name of Tribal Nation / Tribal College / BIE School] seeks to acquire, install, and operate an on-campus AI Server Infrastructure Node to provide local GPU compute capabilities, support STEM degree programs, and protect Indigenous Data Sovereignty by keeping tribal datasets locally hosted under tribal jurisdiction; and

**WHEREAS**, various federal agencies (including the National Science Foundation, U.S. Department of Education, USDA, and Department of Energy), state agencies, and private technology entities (such as NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Google.org) offer non-repayable grant opportunities to fund hardware procurement, network infrastructure, and STEM curriculum;

---

### **NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:**

1. **Authorization**: The Tribal Council hereby formally authorizes and endorses the submission of grant applications to federal, state, private, and corporate funding entities for the purpose of acquiring, deploying, and maintaining on-campus AI server hardware and STEM laboratory infrastructure.

2. **Designation of Lead Official**: The Tribal Council hereby designates [Insert Name and Title of Lead Official, e.g., President of Tribal College / Executive Director of Tribal Education Department] as the Authorized Organization Representative (AOR) empowered to negotiate, sign, and execute all grant agreements, contracts, and reporting documents associated with these funding applications.

3. **Data Sovereignty Affirmation**: The Tribal Council hereby re-affirms that all artificial intelligence models, cultural archives, native language recordings, and sensitive tribal datasets hosted on the funded server infrastructure shall remain the sole and exclusive intellectual property of the [Name of Tribal Nation], protected under tribal sovereignty and data governance protocols.

4. **Matching & In-Kind Support**: [If applicable] The Tribal Council authorizes the commitment of [Insert Amount or "In-Kind Facility Space and Electrical Utilities"] as institutional matching support required by specific grant guidelines.
