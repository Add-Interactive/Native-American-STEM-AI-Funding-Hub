## Executive Summary

Building on-campus AI compute infrastructure is a critical imperative for Native American Tribal Nations, Alaska Native Corporations/Villages, Native Hawaiian Organizations, Tribal Colleges and Universities (TCUs), Native American-Serving Nontribal Institutions (NASNTIs), and Bureau of Indian Education (BIE) / Tribal K-12 schools. Local, on-campus AI servers provide two crucial advantages:
1. **Indigenous Data Sovereignty**: Ensuring sacred cultural datasets, tribal native language archives, environmental data, and sovereign governance records remain securely hosted locally under tribal jurisdiction without unapproved third-party cloud mining.
2. **Computational Equity**: Giving Native students, researchers, and educators direct access to high-performance GPUs for deep learning, large language model fine-tuning, computer vision, GIS land management, and STEM workforce training.

## 🏔️ Heavy Native County & Borough Grants Directory

### 1. ALASKA BOROUGHS & CENSUS AREAS
* **North Slope Borough (Utqiaġvik/Barrow)**: North Slope Borough School District STEM Infrastructure Grants & Arctic Compute Fund ($100K - $500K). Equips Inupiat schools and Ilisaġvik College with Arctic-grade server racks and NVMe storage.
* **Yukon-Koyukuk & Bethel Boroughs**: REAA District Rural Connectivity & Micro-Server Grants ($50K - $300K). Provides off-grid Yup'ik and Athabascan village schools with satellite-buffered micro AI servers.

### 2. ARIZONA COUNTIES
* **Navajo & Apache Counties (Navajo & Hopi Nations)**: Salt River Project (SRP) STEM Classroom Grants & APS Foundation STEM Grants ($5,000 - $25,000 per school). Directly funds computer lab equipment and AI coding kits in Window Rock, Chinle, Kayenta, and Hopi villages.
* **Coconino County (Flagstaff, Tuba City, Supai)**: Coconino County Educational Service Agency (CCESA) Innovation & Server Grants ($15,000 - $75,000) for Havasupai, Hualapai, and Navajo schools.

### 3. NEW MEXICO COUNTIES
* **San Juan & McKinley Counties (Gallup, Shiprock, Zuni)**: LANL Foundation Tribal STEM Grants & San Juan County Educational Allocations ($20,000 - $100,000). Equips Navajo Nation and Zuni Pueblo schools with AI workstations and computer science labs.
* **Sandoval & Rio Arriba Counties (Pueblos & Jicarilla Apache)**: Northern NM STEAM Coalition & Sandoval County Grants ($15,000 - $80,000) for Jemez, Zia, Santo Domingo Pueblos, and Jicarilla Apache youth centers.

### 4. NORTH CAROLINA COUNTIES
* **Swain & Jackson Counties (Eastern Band of Cherokee)**: Cherokee Preservation Foundation STEM & Tech Infrastructure Grants ($25,000 - $150,000). Provides capital for Cherokee Central Schools and language recording/digitizing servers.
* **Robeson County (Lumbee Tribe)**: Robeson County Education Foundation Lumbee STEM Grants ($10,000 - $50,000) for computer labs and robotics hardware.

### 5. OKLAHOMA COUNTIES
* **Cherokee & Adair Counties (Cherokee Nation)**: Cherokee Nation Education Department Tech Grants ($20,000 - $100,000) for schools in Tahlequah and surrounding Cherokee County.
* **Choctaw & McCurtain Counties (Choctaw Nation)**: Choctaw Nation Youth STEM & Tech Lab Grants ($15,000 - $75,000) for southeastern OK reservation schools.

### 6. MONTANA COUNTIES
* **Glacier & Lake Counties (Blackfeet & Flathead CSKT)**: Glacier & Lake County Educational Alliance Grants ($15,000 - $80,000) equipping Salish Kootenai College and Browning/Blackfeet public schools with computer servers.

### 7. SOUTH DAKOTA COUNTIES
* **Oglala Lakota & Todd Counties (Pine Ridge & Rosebud)**: Lakota STEM Infrastructure Grants & Red Cloud Indian School Tech Fund ($15,000 - $80,000) providing server nodes and Lakota language processing hardware.

## 🏛️ State-by-State Indigenous STEM & AI Infrastructure Breakdown

### 1. ALASKA (Alaska Native & Rural Village Focus)
* **U.S. Dept. of Education Alaska Native Education (ANE) Program**: $500,000 to $2,000,000.
* **Denali Commission Rural Alaska Infrastructure & Tech Grants**: $250,000 to $1,500,000.
* **USDA NIFA Alaska Native-Serving & Native Hawaiian-Serving (ANNH) Program**: $150,000 to $800,000.

### 2. HAWAII (Native Hawaiian Focus)
* **U.S. Dept. of Education Native Hawaiian Education Program (NHEP)**: $300,000 to $1,200,000.
* **Office of Hawaiian Affairs (OHA) Community STEM Grants**: $50,000 to $250,000.

### 3. ARIZONA
* **Arizona Commerce Authority (ACA) Tribal Broadband & STEM Tech Grant**: $250,000 to $1,500,000.
* **AZ Dept. of Education Indian Education STEM Fund**: $50,000 to $200,000.

### 4. NEW MEXICO
* **NM Tribal Infrastructure Fund (TIF) & Capital Outlay**: $500,000 to $3,000,000.
* **NM Higher Education Department TCU Tech Innovation Grant**: Direct state equipment subsidies.

### 5. CALIFORNIA
* **CPUC California Tribal Broadband & Tech Capacity Grants**: 100% grant funding ($150K - $1M).
* **California Community Colleges Native American Student Success Fund**: $100K - $400K.

### 6. OKLAHOMA
* **Oklahoma Tribal-State Educational Technology Grants**: $100K - $500K.

### 7. WASHINGTON
* **OSPI Office of Native Education STEM Grant**: $75,000 to $350,000.

### 8. MONTANA
* **Montana OPI Indian Education for All (IEFA) Tech Grants**: $100,000 to $600,000.

### 9. MINNESOTA
* **MN Indian Education Aid & Tribal College Tech Grant**: $80,000 to $450,000.
