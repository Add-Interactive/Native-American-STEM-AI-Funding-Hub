# Add Interactive Studio | Native American STEM & AI Server Funding Hub

Welcome to the **Add Interactive Studio Native American STEM & AI Infrastructure Funding Hub**. This resource was built to provide tribal leaders, educators, grant writers, IT administrators, and researchers with full visibility into federal, state, county, and private/corporate funding streams available to build on-campus AI servers, compute labs, and STEM infrastructure across Tribal Colleges and Universities (TCUs), Native American-Serving Nontribal Institutions (NASNTIs), and K-12 Tribal/BIE schools.

## 🚀 How to Launch the Interactive Dashboard

### Method 1: Double-Click the Launcher
Double-click `launch-dashboard.bat` in this folder. It will instantly launch `index.html` in your default browser.

### Method 2: Open `index.html` Directly
Right-click `index.html` -> **Open with** -> Select **Chrome**, **Edge**, **Firefox**, or any modern browser.

## 🎨 Color-Coded Funding Structure

The dashboard and guide organize all funding streams into intuitive color-coded tiers:

- 🔵 **Federal Grants (Blue/Cyan)**: High-yield grants from NSF, Dept of Education, USDA, BIE, DOE, and FCC.
- 🟣 **Tech Corporate & AI Grants (Purple/Indigo)**: Equipment, GPU, and cloud grants from NVIDIA, Microsoft, Google, Intel, Micron.
- 🟢 **Philanthropic & Foundations (Emerald/Teal)**: Grants from Alfred P. Sloan, AISES, Native Americans in Philanthropy.
- 🟠 **State & Tribal Grants (Amber/Orange)**: State-level grants (AZ, NM, CA, OK, WA, MT, AK, MN) and Tribal Council allocations.
- 🔴 **County & Local Grants (Rose/Red)**: Regional infrastructure, library, and county STEM initiatives.

## 💡 Key Highlights

1. **AI Infrastructure Focus**: Clear tagging for grants that cover local hardware (GPU servers, workstations, storage nodes, network switches) for Native American campuses and schools.
2. **Data Sovereignty Ready**: Guidance on maintaining tribal ownership and privacy of AI models, cultural data, and research datasets.
3. **Step-by-Step Procedure Playbook**: Step-by-step guides for SAM.gov UEI registration, CAGE code, Tribal Council Resolutions, vendor quote gathering, and post-award compliance.
4. **Interactive Hardware Calculator**: Estimate exact server costs (GPUs, RAM, Storage, UPS, Rack Enclosure) and map them to eligible grant opportunities.
