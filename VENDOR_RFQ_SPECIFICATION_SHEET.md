## 1. Technical Requirements Summary

The issuing institution requests formal price quotes for an enterprise GPU server system, high-speed storage, networking, and power backup to be deployed in a grant-funded academic AI laboratory.

### Component 1: Enterprise GPU Server Node (Quantity: 1)
* **Form Factor**: 4U Rackmount Server Chassis with redundant hot-swap power supplies.
* **Processors**: Dual AMD EPYC 9004 Series or Dual Intel Xeon Scalable 4th/5th Gen Processors (Min. 32 Cores per CPU).
* **System Memory**: Minimum 512GB DDR5 4800MHz ECC Registered RAM (Expandable to 1TB+).
* **GPUs**: Minimum 4x NVIDIA L40S 48GB or 4x NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada 48GB GPUs (with NVLink or PCIe Gen5 high-speed interconnect).
* **Internal OS Storage**: Dual 960GB NVMe SSD (RAID 1 Mirror for OS).
* **Power Supplies**: 80 Plus Titanium Redundant (2+2 or 3+1) Power Supply Units.
* **Warranty**: 3-Year to 5-Year 24x7 On-Site Next Business Day Hardware Support.

### Component 2: High-Speed Storage Array (Quantity: 1)
* **Type**: NVMe All-Flash Direct Attach or NAS Storage Array.
* **Usable Capacity**: Minimum 32TB to 64TB Usable NVMe Gen5 Storage in RAID 5/6 configuration.
* **Interface**: 100GbE / PCIe Gen5 SFP28 connection to host server.

### Component 3: Campus Network Interconnect (Quantity: 1)
* **Switch**: 16-Port 100GbE Managed Switch (SFP28 / QSFP28).
* **Cabling**: 4x 100G Direct Attach Copper (DAC) Cables or Transceivers.

### Component 4: Rack Enclosure & Power Backup (Quantity: 1)
* **UPS**: 6000VA / 6000W Online Double-Conversion Rackmount UPS with Network Management Card.
* **Rack**: 42U Enclosure with perforated front/rear doors and cable management.
